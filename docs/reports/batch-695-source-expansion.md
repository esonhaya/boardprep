## Portfolio and review controls

- New-item targets sum to 276 exactly. The target is a coverage goal, not authority to retain unsupported or invalid items.
- Difficulty plan is 1 easy, 4 medium, 1 hard for six-item concepts; the four-item clusters use 1/2/1. Architecture additions are medium application decisions. Actual values are recorded and audited after authoring.
- Each new item carries its proposition/competency, source IDs, lesson material and section, unique applied scenario, explanation, and four distinct choices. Production eligibility is confirmed by `QuestionEligibilityService` and `BoardViewService`.
- Per-item automated source/format/uniqueness checks do not certify answer validity or replace independent subject-expert review. Any ambiguous or unsupported item is excluded from the eligible count and listed in the review queue.
- CHED CMO 76/77/79/80/82 and DepEd RA 11650 IRR PDFs were scanned; their fine-grained text was not machine-extracted. No new item depends on a claim attributed only to those unread pages.
