# Batch 694 Values Education Academic Audit

## Scope and outcome

Reviewed the 35 pre-existing Values Education questions (IDs 5818–5852) and authored 65 new scenario items. The active selector now has 100 unique eligible questions across the three existing Values taxonomy topics and seven concepts. All 100 link to the published Values lesson, a named instructional section, downloadable PDF material, and question-level source references.

**35 of 35 legacy items had confirmed authoring defects and were reauthored.** The 2,389 heuristic cues previously reported for the whole question bank were not treated as 2,389 confirmed defects. After the revisions, the re-run whole-bank distractor audit reports 2,405 heuristic review cues and zero deterministic duplicate-choice errors. Its cues are not semantic judgments.

The 35 legacy items used the same generic instructional stem patterns and repeated the same three wrong-answer ideas: choose what is familiar, apply an unrelated rule, or treat a surface feature as decisive. Those options were weak independently of a particular subject principle, and the keyed answer often won through plainly favorable wording. The revision replaces the abstract prompts with concrete educational, family, community, digital, and professional dilemmas. It uses new answer wording and scenario-specific plausible alternatives; answer text, not a numeric key, is stored in the question records. Every existing ID and learner-usage counter, bookmark, report, and timestamp was retained.

## New content and source method

The 65 new items span those competencies plus dilemmas involving academic integrity, access, classroom dialogue, privacy, public participation, environmental practice, and work ethics. Correct-option positions across all 100 Values questions are balanced 25/25/25/25. The automated distractor-shape audit flags none of these 100 questions for exact or near duplicates, extreme option length, answer-format outliers, repeated distractor text, or the conservative three-negative-option cue. This result only covers those detectable structures.

The lesson is organized around the four Values Education units in the PRC Professional Teachers TOS: Foundations of Values Education, Personhood Development, Transformative Education, and Work Ethics. Original explanations use the current TOS and linked DepEd/teacher-ethics references without reproducing source text. The official DepEd MATATAG page is cited as a curriculum-guide index; its linked guide PDF body was not inspected during this batch. The TOS and curriculum links establish scope and context, not independent academic approval of each item.

Official references: [PRC Professional Teachers TOS](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2025-11%20published%20PROFESSIONAL%20TEACHERS.pdf), [DepEd MATATAG curriculum index](https://www.deped.gov.ph/matatagcurriculumk147/), [DepEd ESEP curriculum guide](https://lrmds.deped.gov.ph/detail/15800), and [DepEd Order 42, s. 2017](https://www.deped.gov.ph/2017/08/11/do-42-s-2017-national-adoption-and-implementation-of-the-philippine-professional-standards-for-teachers/).
