## Values Education audit

The audit reviewed all 35 legacy Values items and authored 65 new scenario questions. The 35 legacy records had generic stems, repeated interchangeable wrong-answer ideas, and keyed options that often signaled themselves through favorable wording. They were rewritten into concrete dilemmas spanning dignity and agency, conflicting duties, contextual values, learner-centered decisions, family responsibilities, civic fairness, peace and gender dignity, environmental stewardship, digital citizenship, professional integrity, and community participation. The question IDs and protected usage counters, bookmarks, reports, and timestamps were preserved.

The 100 keyed answers are balanced 25/25/25/25 across answer positions. The structural distractor audit detects no duplicate or near-duplicate options, extreme length or formatting cues, repeated option strings, or the three-negative-option cue in these 100 items. This does not establish that each item has only one defensibly best answer; cultural interpretation, ambiguity, and curriculum fit remain for human academic review.

The earlier 2,389 whole-bank heuristic cues were review candidates, not confirmed defects. The latest audit reports 2,405 cues across the full bank, with zero deterministic structural errors. The net cue count increased because the larger corpus creates additional opportunities for repetitive wording patterns; no heuristic cue is described as a confirmed defect without item-level inspection. See [`batch-694-values-audit.md`](../reports/batch-694-values-audit.md) and [`batch-694-academic-quality-after.md`](../reports/batch-694-academic-quality-after.md).

The Values lesson is organized around the four Values Education units in the PRC Professional Teachers TOS: Foundations of Values Education, Personhood Development, Transformative Education, and Work Ethics. It includes ethical decision structures, worked dilemmas, misconceptions, practice, and source crosswalks. References include the [PRC Professional Teachers TOS](https://www.prc.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2025-11%20published%20PROFESSIONAL%20TEACHERS.pdf), [DepEd MATATAG curriculum index](https://www.deped.gov.ph/matatagcurriculumk147/), and [DepEd professional standards](https://www.deped.gov.ph/2017/08/11/do-42-s-2017-national-adoption-and-implementation-of-the-philippine-professional-standards-for-teachers/). The MATATAG index was inspected; its linked guide PDF body was not inspected during this batch.
